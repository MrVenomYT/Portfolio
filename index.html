COMMON QUESTIONS

FAQS
Everything You Need to Know About Collaboration & Services
01

What technologies and stack do you specialize in?

I specialize in the MERN Stack (MongoDB, Express.js, React.js, Node.js), JavaScript ES6+/TypeScript, HTML5, CSS3/Sass/Bootstrap, and Discord.js v14 for custom bot development. I also architect scalable RESTful APIs, real-time WebSockets, JWT authentication pipelines, and cloud hosting on Vercel, Render, and Cloudflare.

02

Can you build custom Discord bots with advanced features?

Yes! I engineer high-performance Discord bots with slash commands, automated moderation, interactive ticket panels, leveling & economy systems, music playback, external API integrations, webhooks, and 24/7 uptime hosting setup.

03

How does your project development process work?

The process is structured and transparent: 1) Discovery & Scope: We clarify your objectives and requirements. 2) Architecture & Design: Wireframes and database schema planning. 3) Agile Development: Regular staging demos and milestone updates. 4) Quality Assurance: Cross-browser and responsive testing. 5) Launch & Handover: Production deployment and documentation.

04

What is your typical turnaround time for web apps and bots?

Standard responsive landing pages and single-page apps typically take 3–7 days. Medium-sized full-stack MERN web applications and multi-feature Discord bots usually take 2–4 weeks depending on scope, database complexity, and third-party API integrations.

05

Do you provide post-launch maintenance and support?

Yes, every delivery includes a 30-day warranty for bug fixes and adjustments. I also offer monthly support retainers for ongoing feature development, bot uptime monitoring, dependency upgrades, and performance tuning.

06

How can we kick off a new project together?

You can send a message through the Contact page, email me directly at t3ch91cal.h3r0@gmail.com, or reach out on Discord at MrVenomYT. I typically respond within 24 hours to schedule a consultation or provide an estimate.
